# Code of conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss technical disagreements on their merits and accept review without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory behavior, threats, disclosure of private information and persistent disruption are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content, limit participation or suspend access when necessary to keep collaboration constructive. Responses should reflect the severity, context and recurrence of the behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to [Cevat Batuhan Tolon](mailto:cevatbatuhan.tolon@gmail.com). Include links, dates and enough context to investigate. Reports are shared only with people needed to handle the incident; do not post private details in public issues. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

Depending on the incident, maintainers may issue a warning, remove content, restrict participation temporarily or impose a permanent ban. Affected participants may request reconsideration through the same email address. Maintainers involved in an incident should not decide its outcome when another uninvolved maintainer is available.

Maintainers are subject to the same standards as other participants.
